Why Openeye

Cameras are everywhere. Intelligence isn't.

There are over 1 billion surveillance cameras installed worldwide. That is roughly one camera for every eight people on earth. Yet most of them do nothing but record. The footage only gets reviewed after something has already gone wrong.

The cost of that gap is measured in lives. The International Labour Organization estimates nearly 3 million workers die every year from work-related accidents and diseases, with over 395 million non-fatal workplace injuries annually. Behind almost every incident is a warning that a camera saw, but no one was watching.

Traditional camera AI can only detect what it was expensively trained to detect: a helmet, a person, a vehicle. Teaching it one new thing means collecting a dataset, retraining a model, and hiring an engineer. For most workplaces, that is simply out of reach.

Closer to home

Sri Lanka reports around 4,000 workplace accidents every year, costing an estimated 600,000 lost working days. In November 2025, a tea-factory worker was dragged into a rolling machine that was missing its mandatory safety guard. In the same week, another worker died when a decades-old machine exploded at a rubber plant. Nobody was watching those machines at the moment it mattered.

Openeye turns cameras a business already owns into intelligent safety monitors. No dataset. No training. No engineer. A supervisor types what matters in plain language, and Openeye watches for exactly that, every second of every shift, escalating the moment it sees a violation with the evidence frame attached.

There are a billion cameras watching the world. Almost none of them understand what they see. Openeye changes that, one sentence at a time.

How it works

From camera to alert in four steps

No training pipelines. No labelers. Just connect a camera, draw a zone, and describe what should trigger an alert.

Plug in any camera or video file

Openeye reads from USB webcams, RTSP streams, or uploaded MP4 files. The built-in demo mode lets you try it instantly without any hardware.

  • USB webcams and IP cameras
  • Pre-recorded video clips
  • Built-in animated demo scene

Draw zones directly on the feed

Mark the areas that matter. A machine perimeter, a restricted hallway, or a PPE checkpoint. Rules only evaluate inside the zones you define.

  • Rectangles, polygons, and circles
  • Resize and move zones anytime
  • One zone, many rules

Describe the rule in plain English

No code, no YAML, no model training. Type exactly what you want to detect and Qwen3-VL understands the scene for you.

  • "Alert if a person enters the danger zone"
  • "Call supervisor when a forklift crosses the yellow line"
  • "Flag anyone near the machine without a hard hat"

Receive instant, contextual alerts

When a rule matches, Openeye saves the frame, scores the detection, and sends a Telegram notification to the right contact. No spam.

  • Evidence frame attached
  • Per-zone contact routing
  • Smart deduplication

How to start

Built for builders and operators

Quickstart

Run with Docker

docker compose up -d
# Open http://localhost:5001

One command and you have a working dashboard, detector, and alert pipeline.

Rule syntax

Write rules in English

"Alert if a person enters the danger zone"
"Flag missing hard hat in the loading bay"
"Call supervisor when forklift crosses line"

Openeye uses Qwen3-VL to interpret the scene against your description.

Integration

Connect your services

DASHSCOPE_API_KEY=...
TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=...
TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID=...

Add your Dashscope key for vision understanding, then add Telegram contacts and assign them to zones.
